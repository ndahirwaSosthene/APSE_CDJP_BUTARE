APSE 0.8.1 — WINDOWS 10/11 64-BIT
1. Kanda iburyo kuri ZIP > Extract All. Ntuyitangirize imbere muri ZIP.
2. Fungura folder APSE_CDJP_Butare.
3. Gukoresha portable: kanda START_APSE_WINDOWS.cmd. Setup si ngombwa.

5. Console igume ifunguye. Fungura http://127.0.0.1:8766/ nyuma y’uko server yatangiye.
